Privacy Policy

We don’t collect your data. Here’s the long version.

Effective September 25, 2026 · Applies to Light Tap for iPhone and Apple Watch, version 1.0 and later until this page says otherwise.

No data collectedNothing personal, nothing anonymous, nothing at all leaves your device.
No accounts or sign-inThere is nothing to sign up for.
No ads, analytics or trackingNo third-party SDKs of any kind are in the app.
No network connectionsThe app works entirely offline and never contacts a server.

1. Who we are

Light Tap (“the app”) is developed and published by Sono Studios, an independent studio. Contact: ranestormz@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data or any device data. It contains no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code. It makes no network requests of any kind.

3. Data stored on your device only

So that the app can remember how you like it, a small amount of information is saved in the app’s own private storage on your device. This never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to anyone else.

WhatWhyWhere it lives
Which world you chose (Dusk, Aurora, Fireflies or Tide) So the app opens in the world you left it in App preferences
Your settings: sound on/off, haptics on/off, haptic strength, pace Remembering your preferences App preferences
Whether you have seen the one-screen intro So it only appears once App preferences
A running count of how many lights you have popped The single lifetime stat shown at the bottom of Settings App preferences

None of this identifies you. There is no name, email, account, or device identifier in it. The iPhone app and the Apple Watch app each keep their own copy on their own device; nothing is synced between them, and the app does not use iCloud. iOS and watchOS may include this storage in your device backups under the standard backup rules you control in Settings.

Deleting it: delete the app. The system removes the app’s container and preferences with it. There is no server-side copy to request or delete.

4. Motion

The app can read your device’s motion sensors to tilt the background slightly as you move the device — a visual parallax effect, nothing more. Motion data is used on-device, in real time, for that effect alone. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted, and the app has no other use for it. iOS will ask your permission the first time, and the app works perfectly well if you decline; you can change your mind at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness. If you have Reduce Motion turned on, the app does not use motion at all.

5. Sound

The app plays ambient audio and tap sounds through your device’s speaker or headphones. It does not use the microphone and has no microphone permission. Audio is configured to mix with whatever you are already playing rather than interrupt it, and it respects your silent switch.

6. Apple

You download the app through the App Store, which is operated by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. If you have chosen in Settings to share analytics and crash reports with app developers, Apple may make aggregated, anonymised crash and usage statistics available to us through App Store Connect; that data is collected and controlled by Apple, not by the app, and contains no personal information. You can turn it off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

7. Children

The app has no purchases, no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising and no data collection, so it is suitable for children and complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws by design: it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. Its App Store age rating is shown on the product page.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or erase. If you are in the EU/EEA, UK, California, or anywhere else with a data-protection law, your rights are fully respected simply because no processing takes place. If you have a question or concern, email ranestormz@gmail.com and a person will answer.

9. Future features

The app currently has no purchases and no network access. If that ever changes — for example an optional paid pack of extra worlds, which would be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — this policy will be updated before the feature ships, and the change will be described in the App Store release notes for the version it applies to.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

App Store privacy details, for reference

Apple’s App Privacy label for Light Tap: Data Not Collected. Tracking: none. Third-party SDKs: none. The app ships a privacy manifest declaring one required-reason API — reading and writing its own settings on your device (reason CA92.1).
